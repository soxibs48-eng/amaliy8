## ✨ Key Features & Components

1. **Floating Pill Navigation Bar**:
   - Signature pill geometry with smooth glassmorphism.
   - Quick navigation to *How It Works*, *For Doctors*, *For Hospitals*, *Our Mission*, and *Earnings Calculator*.
   - Mobile responsive drawer with accessible toggle.

2. **Hero Section**:
   - Display typography: *"Veterinary staffing done right."*
   - Playful, hand-drawn vector illustration of a veterinarian on a skateboard with joyful animal companions.
   - Dual action CTAs with verified clinical trust metrics (150,000+ shifts filled, 2,000+ partner hospitals, top 1-in-6 vetting).

3. **Floating Overlap Action Cards**:
   - **For Veterinarians**: Guaranteed W-2 salary, complete schedule flexibility, full healthcare benefits.
   - **For Hospitals**: Rigorously vetted relief coverage, VetFit™ hospital matching, zero placement fees.

4. **Interactive Shift & Salary Estimator**:
   - Interactive slider adjusting days worked per week (1 to 5 days).
   - Clinical specialty switcher: General Practice (GP) vs Emergency / Urgent Care (ER).
   - Real-time calculations of guaranteed base salary, PTO days, CE allowance, and 401(k) retirement match.

5. **"How IndeVets Works" (3-Step Roadmap)**:
   - **Step 1**: *"We hire great vets."* (The 1-in-6 vetting standard, medical autonomy).
   - **Step 2**: *"We partner with the best workplaces."* (VetFit™ quality clinic criteria).
   - **Step 3**: *"Happy vets. Happy hospitals. Better medicine."* (Combating veterinary burnout).

6. **"WHO WE ARE / Our Mission"**:
   - Whimsical parade illustration of animals and veterinarian marching with banner.
   - Mission statement combating compassion fatigue and building sustainable veterinary careers.
   - Dynamic animated counter for key community milestones.

7. **Community Voices & Testimonials**:
   - Interactive tabbed switch between doctor feedback and hospital director reviews.
   - Verified ratings and credentialed reviews.

8. **Call-To-Action Banner**:
   - Full-width bright lime banner: *"Sounds pretty good, right? Let's do this."*
   - Vintage cruiser bicycle illustration with happy dog in basket.
   - Dedicated pathways for doctors applying and hospitals requesting coverage.

9. **Modals & Lead Capture**:
   - Doctor Application Modal with multi-field validation.
   - Hospital Partnership Request Modal.
   - Animated toast notification feedback.

10. **Master Footer**:
    - Complete site directory, Philadelphia headquarters contact info, phone, email, and legal links.
