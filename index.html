LET'S LOOK AT THE WHOLE PICTURE

How IndeVets Works

Step one...

We hire great vets.

Fewer than 1 in 6 applicants make the cut. We rigorously screen for clinical excellence, emotional intelligence, and bed-side compassion. Every IndeVet is employed as a salaried W-2 team member with guaranteed earnings, full healthcare, and the psychological safety to practice great medicine autonomously.

Step two...

We partner with the best workplaces.

We don't send our doctors anywhere we wouldn't want to work ourselves. Each hospital completes our comprehensive VetFit™ onboarding evaluation to verify modern medical equipment, safe technician-to-doctor ratios, respectful practice cultures, and transparent communication protocols.

The result?

Happy vets. Happy hospitals. Better medicine.

Overworked hospital teams get dependable relief. Doctors rediscover their love for veterinary medicine without burnout or administrative headaches. And pets receive compassionate, uninterrupted medical care from rested, energized professionals who truly love what they do.

Our Mission

The veterinary industry has faced an epidemic of burnout, mental fatigue, and compassion fatigue. We started IndeVets to fundamentally change that — by building a sustainable career model where veterinarians control their lives, clinics get reliable coverage without the chaos, and patients always come first.
